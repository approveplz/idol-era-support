Idol Era

A K-pop idol story game for iPhone by Alan Zhang.

Support

You are the newest member of a fictional idol group. Spend energy on posts, lives, schedules and messages to grow your fandom and finish each comeback. Energy refills every day at 4am.

  • Restore purchases: Shop tab, then Restore purchases at the bottom.
  • Cancel the Era Pass: Shop tab, then Manage subscription, or iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.
  • Progress: your game is saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes local story progress. Energy, gems and verified purchases belong to your app-specific Apple identity and can be recovered using the same Apple Account.
  • Terms of Use: Apple's standard license agreement.

For help or feedback, email alan@inversionconsultants.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026.

On your iPhone. Your story progress is saved on your iPhone. Pending actions are also saved so the app can recover a result after a connection failure without spending energy twice.

Your wallet. Apple supplies a signed identifier specific to your Apple Account and Idol Era. The server verifies it and stores a derived identifier with your wallet, energy, gems, rewards, subscription status and purchase transaction IDs. We do not receive your Apple Account email or password. These records remain to preserve balances and prevent repeated purchase delivery.

Writing your story. The app sends your stage name, group name, choices, typed posts, messages and answers, and a summary of your game to the Idol Era server on Fly.io in the United States. It forwards that text to Anthropic (Claude), without your wallet identifier or Apple proof. A generated reply is stored until your app confirms it has saved the result. It may repeat text you typed. Operation identifiers and input hashes remain to prevent duplicate charges. Failed output checks may put short excerpts in error logs. Anthropic processes text under its commercial terms, which do not permit training on it, and normally deletes it within 30 days, or up to 2 years if safety systems flag it. Please do not type personal information into the game.

Your consent. You agree before your debut. To stop further story requests, stop playing and delete the app. Deleting the app does not delete the server wallet, purchase records or a reply still awaiting confirmation. Contact support about retained data.

Usage records. For each model call the server records its time, wallet ID, app build, request kind, model, token use and estimated cost. These records help us monitor service costs and activity. Usage records are kept for 90 days and are linked to your app-specific wallet.

Reports. Reporting AI content removes it from your game, except agency decisions that you still need to answer. The app sends the reported text, its location and your wallet ID to the server for review. Reports may repeat words you typed and are kept for 90 days. To request earlier deletion, email support with the reported text.

Checking requests. The app sends a fresh Apple DeviceCheck token, which the server checks with Apple and does not store. Apple signed app and purchase proofs are verified but not stored as raw proofs. Your IP address limits simultaneous requests from one network. Those counts last only while requests run and the server does not store your IP.

Purchases. Apple handles payment. We do not receive card or bank details. The server checks current purchase and subscription information with Apple, and keeps transaction identifiers and delivery records so a retry cannot charge energy or grant a purchase twice.

Children. Idol Era is for ages 13 and up and is not directed to children under 13. Where a state law requires it, the app asks Apple for your age range when it opens, uses it only to keep out players under 13, and never saves it or sends it off your iPhone.

Questions. Email alan@inversionconsultants.com. If you contact support, your email address and message are used only to answer you. Changes to this policy will be posted on this page.
