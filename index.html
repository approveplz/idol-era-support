Idol Era

A K-pop idol story game for iPhone by Alan Zhang.

Support

You are the newest member of a fictional idol group. Spend energy on posts, lives, schedules and messages to grow your fandom and finish each comeback. Energy refills every day at 4am.

  • Restore purchases: Shop tab, then Restore purchases at the bottom.
  • Cancel the Era Pass: Shop tab, then Manage subscription, or iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.
  • Progress: your game is saved on your iPhone. There is no account, so deleting the app deletes your game.
  • Terms of Use: Apple's standard license agreement.

For help or feedback, email alan@inversionconsultants.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026.

On your iPhone. Your game is saved in a file on your iPhone. The app also creates a random device ID the first time it opens and keeps it on your iPhone. It is not your name, Apple Account, or advertising identifier.

Writing your story. The game's characters and news are written by AI. When you act in the game, the app sends what the story needs to the Idol Era server, which runs on Fly.io in the United States: your stage name, your choices, what you type in posts, messages and answers, and a summary of your game. The server forwards that text, without your device ID, to Anthropic (Claude) to write the story. It does not keep the text, except a short excerpt in its error log when an AI reply fails a check, and what you report (below). Anthropic processes the text under its commercial terms, which do not let it train AI models on the text, protects it at least as well as this policy does, and deletes it within 30 days (up to 2 years if its safety systems flag it). Please don't type personal information into the game.

Your consent. You agree to this before your debut. To withdraw it, stop playing and delete the app. Nothing more is sent after that. What the server already keeps is deleted after 90 days (below).

Usage records. For each request the server records the time, your device ID, the kind of request, the AI model, its size, and its estimated cost. These records enforce daily limits and count how many players are active. They are kept for 90 days. They are not linked to your name or Apple Account. The app never shows its random device ID, so support can't find your records on request.

Reports. You can report any post, comment, story, message, scene or agency decision the AI wrote. The app deletes it from your game (an agency decision stays so you can still answer) and sends the server its text, where it appeared, and your device ID. The text can include words you typed when the story repeats them. The server keeps each report for 90 days so the developer can review it and fix the AI's rules, and limits how many reports one device can send each day. To have a report deleted sooner, email support with the text you reported.

Checking requests. Each request also carries a one-time Apple DeviceCheck token. The server sends it to Apple only to confirm the request comes from the real app on a real Apple device. The token doesn't identify you, and the server doesn't keep it. The server also uses your IP address to limit how many requests one network can make each day. It keeps that count in memory only, never saves your IP address, and forgets it the next day.

Purchases. Apple handles payment. The app never sees your payment details.

Children. Idol Era is for ages 13 and up and is not directed to children under 13. Where a state law requires it, the app asks Apple for your age range when it opens, uses it only to keep out players under 13, and never saves it or sends it off your iPhone.

Questions. Email alan@inversionconsultants.com. If you contact support, your email address and message are used only to answer you. Changes to this policy will be posted on this page.
