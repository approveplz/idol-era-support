Idol Era

A K-pop idol story game for iPhone by Alan Zhang.

Support

You are the newest member of a fictional idol group. Spend energy on posts, lives, schedules and messages to grow your fandom and finish each comeback. Energy refills every day at 4am.

  • Restore purchases: Shop tab, then Restore purchases at the bottom.
  • Cancel the Era Pass: Shop tab, then Manage subscription, or iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.
  • Progress: your game is saved on your iPhone. There is no account, so deleting the app deletes your game.
  • Terms of Use: Apple's standard license agreement.

For help or feedback, email alan@inversionconsultants.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026.

On your iPhone. Your game is saved in a file on your iPhone. The app also creates a random device ID the first time it opens and keeps it on your iPhone. It is not your name, Apple Account, or advertising identifier.

Writing your story. The game's characters and news are written by AI. When you act in the game, the app sends what the story needs to the Idol Era server: your stage name, your choices, what you type in posts, messages and answers, and a summary of your game. The server forwards that text, without your device ID, to Anthropic (Claude) and Google (Gemini) to write the story. It does not keep the text, except a short excerpt in its error log when an AI reply fails a check. Anthropic and Google process it under their own privacy policies. Please don't type personal information into the game.

Usage records. For each request the server records the time, your device ID, the kind of request, the AI model, its size, and its estimated cost. These records enforce daily limits and count how many players are active. They are kept for 90 days. They are not linked to your name or Apple Account. To have the records for your device deleted, email support.

Ads. When you choose to watch a rewarded ad, Google AdMob shows it. AdMob may collect your device identifiers, approximate location from your IP address, and how you interact with ads, as described in Google's privacy policy. The app does not ask to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Purchases. Apple handles payment. The app never sees your payment details.

Children. Idol Era is for ages 13 and up and is not directed to children under 13.

Questions. Email alan@inversionconsultants.com. If you contact support, your email address and message are used only to answer you. Changes to this policy will be posted on this page.
